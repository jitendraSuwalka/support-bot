# Account administration and statements

## Who to contact

For account matters the quickest route is to speak to your own adviser or their team directly. If you do not know who your adviser is, call the switchboard on 0117 496 0100 and we will connect you.

## Updating your details

Change of address, telephone number or email can be made in the client portal under My Details, or by secure message. A change of name needs a certified copy of the marriage certificate, deed poll or decree absolute. A change of bank account for withdrawals needs a signed instruction and a recent bank statement, for your protection.

## Statements and valuations

- Valuation reports are issued quarterly, within 15 working days of the end of March, June, September and December, and published to the portal.
- Annual tax statements and consolidated tax certificates are published by the end of May each year.
- A valuation as at any date can be requested from your adviser's team; it is normally available within two working days.

## Fee queries

Every quarterly report shows the fees charged in the period. If anything is unclear, ask your adviser or their team; a written breakdown is available on request.

## Withdrawals

Withdrawals from a general investment account are usually paid within five working days of the instruction. Withdrawals from a pension depend on your age and the type of pension, and should be discussed with your adviser first because tax may be payable. Withdrawals are paid only to a bank account in your name that we have verified.

## Paper or online

All documents are published to the portal. Paper copies by post can be arranged at no extra charge by asking your adviser's team.
