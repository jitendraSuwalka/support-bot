# Account administration and statements

## Who to contact

For account matters the quickest route is to speak to your own adviser or their team directly. If you do not know who your adviser is, call the switchboard on 0117 496 0100 and we will connect you.

## Updating your details

Change of address, telephone number or email can be made in the client portal under My Details, or by secure message. A change of name needs a certified copy of the marriage or civil partnership certificate, a deed poll, or the final order of divorce or dissolution (called a decree absolute for divorces completed before April 2022) together with your birth certificate.

## Changing the bank account we pay to

For your protection, a change of the bank account we pay withdrawals to needs a signed instruction and a recent statement for the new account showing your name, and we confirm the change with you by telephone on the number we hold before it takes effect. Withdrawals are paid only to a bank account in your name that we have verified in this way.

## Statements and valuations

- Valuation reports are issued quarterly, within 15 working days of the end of March, June, September and December, and published to the portal.
- The annual costs and charges statement, showing in pounds every charge you have paid in the year and its effect on your return, is published each April.
- Annual tax statements and consolidated tax certificates are published by the end of May each year.
- A valuation as at any date can be requested from your adviser's team; it is normally available within two working days.

## Fee queries

Every quarterly report shows the fees charged in the period, and the annual costs and charges statement brings them together. If anything is unclear, ask your adviser or their team; a written breakdown is available on request.

## Withdrawals

From a general investment account or ISA, if the cash is already available we pay a withdrawal within five working days of your instruction. If holdings have to be sold first, sales normally settle within two working days of dealing and we pay within five working days of settlement. Withdrawals from a pension depend on your age and the type of pension; tax may be payable, and the Pensions page explains the options.

## Paper or online

All documents are published to the portal. Paper copies by post can be arranged at no extra charge by asking your adviser's team.
