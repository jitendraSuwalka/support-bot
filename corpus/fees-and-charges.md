# Fees and charges

We are paid by fees agreed with you in advance. We do not receive commission from product providers. All fees are confirmed in writing in your client agreement and services and costs disclosure before any work starts. The figures below are our standard rates.

## VAT

Whether VAT applies depends on the service. Advice that leads to us arranging a product or investment for you is exempt from VAT. Stand-alone advice, and discretionary investment management, are subject to VAT at the standard rate. Your client agreement states which of your fees carry VAT.

## Initial financial planning

A full financial plan costs £2,400, plus VAT where it applies. It covers a review of your whole financial position, a written plan with recommendations, and a meeting to go through it. One-off advice on a single topic, such as how to use allowances before the end of the tax year, costs £1,200, plus VAT where it applies.

## Ongoing advice

Ongoing advice is charged at 0.75% a year of the assets under advice, collected monthly from your accounts. It covers your annual review meeting, access to your adviser throughout the year, rebalancing recommendations and the quarterly reports.

## Discretionary investment management

Where we manage your investments on a discretionary basis, the annual management fee is tiered and is subject to VAT:

- 0.60% a year on the first £1,000,000.
- 0.45% a year on the portion between £1,000,000 and £3,000,000.
- 0.30% a year on the portion above £3,000,000.

Where we provide both ongoing advice and discretionary management, both fees apply. The combined cost is set out in your client agreement and shown in pounds in your annual costs and charges statement.

## Custody and dealing

Custody of your assets by our custodian costs 0.15% a year. There are no dealing charges on our model portfolios, including when holdings are sold to pay a withdrawal or to close an account. Individual share trades on advisory accounts cost £12.50 each.

## Fund costs

The funds we invest in carry their own charges, which are set by the fund managers and deducted within the fund. We select funds partly on cost; the average ongoing fund charge across our model portfolios was 0.21% a year at 30 June 2026.

## Annual costs and charges statement

Each April we send every client a statement showing, in pounds, all the charges paid in the previous year: our fees, custody, dealing and fund charges, and the effect they have had on the return of your portfolio.

## Minimum portfolio

Our discretionary investment management service is designed for portfolios of £250,000 or more. Financial planning is available at any level of wealth.

## What we do not charge for

There is no charge for an introductory meeting, for paper copies of documents, for making a complaint, or for closing or transferring an account.
