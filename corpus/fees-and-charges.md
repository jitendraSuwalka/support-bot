# Fees and charges

We are paid by fees agreed with you in advance. We do not receive commission from product providers. All fees are confirmed in writing in your engagement letter before any work starts. The figures below are our standard rates; VAT is added where it applies.

## Initial financial planning

A full financial plan costs £2,400 plus VAT. It covers a review of your whole financial position, a written plan with recommendations, and a meeting to go through it. One-off advice on a single topic, such as a pension transfer, costs £1,200 plus VAT.

## Ongoing advice

Ongoing advice is charged at 0.75% a year of the assets we advise on, collected monthly from your accounts. It covers your annual review meeting, access to your adviser throughout the year, rebalancing recommendations and the quarterly reports.

## Investment management

Where we manage your investments on a discretionary basis, the annual management fee is tiered:

- 0.60% a year on the first £1,000,000.
- 0.45% a year on the portion between £1,000,000 and £3,000,000.
- 0.30% a year on the portion above £3,000,000.

The investment management fee is charged instead of, not in addition to, the ongoing advice fee where we provide both services.

## Custody and dealing

Custody of your assets by our custodian costs 0.15% a year. There are no dealing charges on our model portfolios. Individual share trades on advisory accounts cost £12.50 each.

## Fund costs

The funds we invest in carry their own charges, which are set by the fund managers and deducted within the fund. We select funds partly on cost; the average ongoing fund charge across our model portfolios was 0.21% a year at 30 June 2026.

## Minimum portfolio

Our discretionary investment management service is designed for portfolios of £250,000 or more. Financial planning is available at any level of wealth.

## What we do not charge for

There is no charge for an introductory meeting, for paper copies of documents, for making a complaint, or for closing or transferring an account.
