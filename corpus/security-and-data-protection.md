# Security, fraud prevention and your data

## What we will never ask for

Fernhill Wealth will never ask you, by phone, email, text or in person, for your full password, your two-step verification codes, your PIN, or your full card details. We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account" or to install software on your computer. Anyone who does is not from Fernhill Wealth.

## How to check a call is from us

If you receive an unexpected call claiming to be from Fernhill Wealth, hang up and call the switchboard on 0117 496 0100 using a number you have looked up yourself. Genuine staff will understand and will be happy to wait.

## Reporting suspected fraud

If you think you have been targeted by fraud involving your Fernhill Wealth account, call the switchboard immediately on 0117 496 0100 and ask for the Fraud and Security team. You can also report fraud to the police through Report Fraud, the national fraud reporting service, at reportfraud.police.uk or on 0300 123 2040. If you live in Scotland, report to Police Scotland on 101.

## Payments out

Withdrawals are only ever paid to a bank account in your name that we have verified against a bank statement. Changes to that account require a signed instruction and a recent statement for the new account, and are confirmed to you by telephone on the number we hold before they take effect.

## Your personal data

We process personal data to provide our services, to meet regulatory obligations, and to prevent crime. Our full privacy notice is on the website. In summary:

- We keep client records for seven years after the relationship ends, or longer where regulation requires.
- You have the right to a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, and in some circumstances to have data deleted. We respond to such requests within one month; for complex requests the law allows us to extend this by up to two further months, and we will tell you if we need to.
- We share personal data only where needed to provide the service or where the law requires: with our custodian and product providers, our regulators, HM Revenue & Customs, fraud prevention agencies, the Financial Services Compensation Scheme, and our professional advisers. We do not sell personal data.
- If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Requests about personal data can be sent to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@fernhillwealth.example or by post to Fernhill House, 22 Harbourside Walk, Bristol, BS1 5XY.

## Recording of calls

Calls to and from the firm may be recorded for regulatory purposes and to help resolve any dispute about an instruction. Recordings are kept for at least five years.
