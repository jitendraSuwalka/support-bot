# Using the client portal

The client portal is where existing clients view valuations, download documents, send secure messages to their adviser and update contact details. It is available 24 hours a day at the client login link on our website and through the Fernhill Wealth app.

## Logging in

You log in with your email address, your password and a six-digit code from your authenticator app or sent by text message. Two-step verification cannot be switched off.

## If you cannot log in

- Forgotten password: use the Forgotten password link on the login page. A reset link is emailed to the address we hold and is valid for 30 minutes.
- No verification code: check that your phone has signal, or use the backup codes issued when you set up two-step verification.
- Account locked: five incorrect attempts lock the account for 30 minutes. If you are still locked out after that, call the portal access helpline on 0117 496 0233, 09:00 to 17:30 on working days.
- Changed phone number: call the portal access helpline so we can re-register your device. For security we will ask you to confirm your identity.

## What you can see

- Current valuation of each account, updated each working day.
- Transactions for the last seven years.
- Quarterly valuation reports, the annual costs and charges statement, annual tax statements and your suitability reports.
- Secure messages to and from your adviser, with a reply within two working days.

## Documents

Documents are published to the portal and retained for at least seven years. If you would prefer paper copies, your adviser can arrange this at no extra charge.

## Security

We never ask for your full password, your two-step verification codes or your card details, by phone, email or text. If anyone asks for them while claiming to be from Fernhill Wealth, end the conversation and call the switchboard on 0117 496 0100.
