# ISAs: a guide for clients

This guide is general information about the rules for Individual Savings Accounts in the 2026/27 tax year, and the changes already announced for April 2027. It is not personal advice. Whether an ISA is right for you depends on your circumstances; your adviser can discuss this with you.

## The allowance

Adults aged 18 or over can pay up to £20,000 into ISAs in the 2026/27 tax year, which runs from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027. The allowance can be split between cash ISAs, stocks and shares ISAs, innovative finance ISAs and a Lifetime ISA, subject to the Lifetime ISA's own limit of £4,000. Any unused allowance does not carry forward.

## Lifetime ISA

A Lifetime ISA can be opened by anyone aged 18 to 39. Up to £4,000 a year can be paid in, counting towards the £20,000 overall allowance, and the government adds a 25% bonus of up to £1,000 a year. Money can be withdrawn without charge to buy a first home costing £450,000 or less, provided the account has been open for at least 12 months, from age 60, or if you are terminally ill with less than 12 months to live. Other withdrawals incur a 25% government charge. The government is consulting on a new first-time-buyer ISA to replace the Lifetime ISA; Lifetime ISAs can still be opened until the new product is available, and existing accounts continue under the current rules.

## Changes from 6 April 2027

- The amount that investors under the age of 65 can pay into cash ISAs each tax year will be capped at £12,000. The overall £20,000 allowance is unchanged, so the remainder can still go into stocks and shares or innovative finance ISAs. Investors aged 65 and over keep a £20,000 cash ISA limit.
- Transfers from stocks and shares or innovative finance ISAs into cash ISAs will no longer be permitted.
- A charge will apply to interest paid on cash held inside a stocks and shares or innovative finance ISA.
- Money already held in cash ISAs is not affected by the cap.

## Transfers

You can transfer an ISA between providers without losing its tax status, provided the transfer is made through the new provider's transfer process rather than by withdrawing the money. Under HM Revenue & Customs' guidelines, transfers between cash ISAs should take no longer than 15 working days and other ISA transfers no longer than 30 calendar days. Re-registering holdings in specie can take longer where fund managers are slow to respond. We do not charge for transfers in or out.

## Flexible ISAs

Our stocks and shares ISA is flexible: money withdrawn during a tax year can be replaced in the same tax year without using more of your allowance.

## Tax and inheritance

Interest, dividends and capital gains within an ISA are free of UK income tax and capital gains tax. ISAs form part of your estate for inheritance tax. A surviving spouse or civil partner is entitled to an additional permitted subscription equal to the value of the deceased's ISAs, in addition to their own allowance, whether or not they inherit the investments themselves. The ISA continues to be tax-free as a continuing account of a deceased investor for up to three years while the estate is administered.

## Important information

This page is general information, not personal advice. Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may change in the future. The value of investments and the income from them can fall as well as rise, and you may get back less than you invested.
