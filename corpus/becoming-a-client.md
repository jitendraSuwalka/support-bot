# Becoming a client: what to expect

## Who we work with

We act for individuals, couples, families, trustees, charities and personal representatives of estates. Our discretionary investment management service is designed for portfolios of £250,000 or more; financial planning is available at any level of wealth. We treat all our clients as retail clients, which gives you the highest level of protection under the Financial Conduct Authority's rules.

## Identity and anti-money-laundering checks

Like every regulated firm, we must verify your identity and understand the source of your wealth before we can act for you. We normally do this electronically from your name, date of birth and address; where that is not possible we ask for a passport or driving licence and a recent utility bill or bank statement. For larger sums we ask for evidence of where the money came from, such as a completion statement, a sale agreement or a statement from the previous provider.

## Joint accounts, trusts and charities

Joint accounts need identity checks for each holder. Trusts need the trust deed and identity checks for each trustee; charities need the governing document and details of the trustees or directors. We register trusts with HMRC's Trust Registration Service where that is required.

## Clients outside the UK

We can act for clients resident outside the United Kingdom in many countries, but not all. We are unable to act for persons who are US citizens or US tax residents. We report account information to HMRC for exchange with other tax authorities where international agreements require it.

## How long it takes

Once we have your signed client agreement and the identity documents we need, accounts are usually open within five working days. Transfers in from other providers then follow the timescales on the Closing or Transferring page.

## Your documents

Before you commit to anything you receive our client agreement, a services and costs disclosure showing what you will pay, our order execution policy, our conflicts of interest policy and our privacy notice. Please read them and ask your adviser about anything that is unclear.

## If your adviser leaves

If your adviser leaves the firm, we introduce you to a new adviser from the same office and your service, fees and investments continue unchanged.
