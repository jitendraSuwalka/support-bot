# Booking an appointment or a call back

## Becoming a client

People considering becoming a client can book a free introductory appointment through the Book an Appointment page or by calling the new client enquiry line on 0808 157 0450. The introductory meeting lasts about 45 minutes, carries no charge and no obligation, and can take place at any of our six offices, by telephone or by video call.

At the introductory meeting we explain how we work, what our services cost, and whether we think we can help. If we both decide to go ahead, we send a written engagement letter setting out the agreed work and fees before anything is charged.

## Existing clients

Existing clients can book a meeting with their adviser by telephone, by email, or through the client portal under Messages. Annual review meetings are scheduled by your adviser and confirmed in writing at least two weeks in advance.

## Requesting a call back

The call-back form on the Contact Us page asks for your name, a telephone number, your preferred date and a preferred time. The times offered run from 09:00 to 17:00 on the hour, with a final 17:45 slot. These are call-back slots, not office opening hours. We aim to return calls within one working day of the chosen slot.

## What to bring

For an introductory meeting you do not need to bring anything. For a planning meeting it helps to have recent statements for any pensions, investments, mortgages and insurance policies, and an idea of your income and regular outgoings. Please never bring or send passwords or PINs.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment

Let us know by telephone or through the portal as early as you can. There is no charge for cancelling or rearranging an appointment.
