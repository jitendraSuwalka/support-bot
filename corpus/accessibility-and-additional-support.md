# Accessibility and additional support

We want every client to be able to understand our service and communicate with us in the way that suits them best. Under the Consumer Duty we take particular care with clients who may be in vulnerable circumstances, whether temporarily or permanently.

## Documents in other formats

Reports, letters and guides are available in large print, in braille, as audio, and as accessible PDF. Tell your adviser or call the switchboard on 0117 496 0100; there is no charge and the format preference is recorded so that you do not need to ask again.

## Communicating with us

- Telephone: if you use Relay UK, dial 18001 before the number you are calling.
- Video calls with a British Sign Language interpreter can be arranged with one week's notice.
- Meetings can be held at your home or by video if visiting an office is difficult.
- If you prefer a particular time of day, a slower pace, or a trusted person to join your meetings, tell us and we will arrange it.

## Our offices

All six offices have step-free access and accessible toilets. Hearing loops are installed in the meeting rooms at Bristol, London and Manchester. Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere.

## Trusted contacts and third parties

With your written permission we can record a trusted contact whom we may call if we are concerned about your wellbeing or cannot reach you. If you want someone to act on your behalf, we can accept a third-party mandate for information only, or a registered power of attorney for instructions.

## Changes in circumstances

If your health, your circumstances or your confidence with financial matters change, please tell us. We can adjust how often we contact you, how we explain things, and the pace at which decisions are taken. Nothing you tell us affects the service you receive except to improve it.

## Feedback

If anything about our service is hard to use, we would like to know. Contact your adviser, the switchboard, or feedback@fernhillwealth.example.
