# Investment management service

## Discretionary and advisory

With discretionary management, you agree an investment mandate and risk level with us and our investment managers make the day-to-day decisions within it. With advisory management, we recommend changes and you decide whether to make each one. Which service suits you depends on how involved you want to be in individual decisions; your adviser will discuss both with you and the choice is recorded in your suitability report.

## Risk profiles

Portfolios are managed to one of seven risk profiles, from 1 (lowest risk, mainly cash and short-dated bonds) to 7 (highest risk, almost entirely equities). Your profile is agreed with your adviser using a questionnaire and a conversation about your capacity for loss, and is reviewed at every annual review.

## How portfolios are built

- A long-term strategic allocation between equities, bonds, property and alternatives for each risk profile, set by our Investment Committee and reviewed annually.
- Active and passive funds selected by our research team on cost, process and performance consistency.
- Rebalancing whenever an asset class drifts more than 5 percentage points from its target, and at least every six months.

## Responsible investment

Each risk profile is available in a version that excludes companies deriving more than 10% of revenue from tobacco, controversial weapons or thermal coal, and that applies our published responsible investment policy when selecting funds. The policy, the exclusion list and how we measure them are set out in our Responsible Investment Policy, available on request and on the website. Fees are the same as for the standard portfolios. These portfolios do not carry a sustainability label under the FCA's Sustainability Disclosure Requirements.

## Reporting

You receive a quarterly valuation report showing holdings, performance against the agreed benchmark, transactions and fees, published to the client portal within 15 working days of each quarter end. If the value of a discretionary portfolio falls by 10% or more since the start of a quarterly reporting period, we tell you by the end of the working day on which it happens, as the rules require. Your investment manager is available to discuss any report.

## Where your assets are held

Your investments are held by our independent custodian through its nominee company. Legal title is registered in the nominee's name; you remain the beneficial owner, your holdings are identified to you in the custodian's records, and they are kept separate from the firm's and the custodian's own assets under the Financial Conduct Authority's client assets rules. If there were a shortfall following the failure of the firm or the custodian, the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may cover eligible claims up to £85,000 per person.

## Risk warning

The value of investments and the income from them can fall as well as rise, and you may get back less than you invested. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns.
