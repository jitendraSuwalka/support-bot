# Financial planning service

Financial planning covers either a comprehensive plan across your whole financial position, or one-off advice on a single topic. All financial planners either hold or are working towards Chartered status.

## What a comprehensive plan covers

- Pensions and retirement planning, including when you could afford to stop work.
- Tax planning, using allowances such as the ISA allowance and pension annual allowance.
- Financial protection: life cover, income protection and critical illness cover.
- Inheritance tax and estate planning, including gifts and trusts.
- Cashflow modelling, which projects your income, spending and assets year by year to show whether your plans are affordable.
- Investing for children, including Junior ISAs.
- Support through life-changing events such as divorce, bereavement or long-term care.

## The process

1. Discovery meeting: we gather information about your circumstances, goals and attitude to risk. Usually 90 minutes.
2. Analysis: we model your position and test it against your goals.
3. The plan: a written report with specific recommendations, presented at a second meeting, usually three to four weeks after discovery.
4. Implementation: if you choose to act on the recommendations, we arrange the products and investments.
5. Ongoing service: an annual review meeting and access to your adviser throughout the year.

## Suitability

Every recommendation is set out in a written suitability report explaining what we recommend, why it is suitable for you, the costs, and the risks. You are never obliged to act on a recommendation.

## One-off advice

One-off advice is available on a single topic, such as whether to consolidate pensions, how to draw retirement income, or how to use allowances before the end of the tax year. It follows the same process on a smaller scale and is confirmed in a suitability report.

## Independence

Fernhill Wealth gives independent advice. We consider products from across the whole market and are not restricted to any provider's range.
