# Support through life events

There is a dedicated help and support route for clients dealing with bereavement, divorce, serious illness, or arranging power of attorney. These situations are handled by a specialist team rather than through the standard enquiry channels. The bereavement and life events team can be reached on 0117 496 0412, 09:00 to 17:30 on working days, or at lifeevents@fernhillwealth.example.

## Bereavement

If a client has died, please let us know as soon as you are able. We will place a hold on the account so that no further fees or trades are taken, and send you a short guide to what happens next. We will need:

- the original or a certified copy of the death certificate;
- details of the executor or administrator of the estate;
- the grant of probate or letters of administration before assets can be released.

A named member of the team will stay with you throughout. Valuations for probate are provided free of charge.

## Divorce or dissolution

We can provide valuations and statements for financial disclosure, and we work with solicitors on the implementation of pension sharing orders. Where both parties are clients, we will arrange separate advisers to avoid any conflict of interest.

## Power of attorney

If you have appointed an attorney under a Lasting Power of Attorney for property and financial affairs, send us a certified copy of the registered document and proof of the attorney's identity. Once registered, the attorney can instruct us within the powers granted. If you would like to put a power of attorney in place, your adviser can explain the options; we recommend that the document itself is drawn up by a solicitor.

## Serious illness

If illness affects your ability to manage your affairs, tell your adviser. We can simplify communications, arrange meetings at home or by video, add a trusted contact to your account with your permission, and review whether your plan still fits your circumstances.

## Financial difficulty

If you are worried about money, your adviser can review your plan and the options available. We will never pressure you to keep investments you need to sell.
